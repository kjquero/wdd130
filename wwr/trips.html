Our Trips

The Scenic Float

Perfect for families and first-timers, the Scenic Float is a relaxing journey down the calm stretches of the river. No experience needed — just sit back, enjoy the stunning Virginia scenery, and let the current carry you. Ideal for ages 5 and up.

  • Difficulty: Beginner
  • Duration: 2 hours
  • Min. Age: 5+
  • Group Size: Up to 12
  • Price: $35 per person

Half-Day Adventure

Ready to feel the rush? The Half-Day Adventure takes you through a mix of moderate rapids, giving you just enough excitement without going overboard. Great for teens and adults looking for their first real whitewater experience. All safety gear and a certified guide included.

  • Difficulty: Intermediate
  • Duration: 4 hours
  • Min. Age: 12+
  • Group Size: Up to 8
  • Price: $65 per person

Full-Day Expedition

Take on a full day on the river with our most popular package. The Full-Day Expedition covers a wide stretch of river, combining calm floats and thrilling rapids. Lunch is included, and our guides will share stories of the river along the way. A complete adventure from sunrise to afternoon.

  • Difficulty: Intermediate
  • Duration: 8 hours
  • Min. Age: 10+
  • Group Size: Up to 10
  • Price: $100 per person

Expert Rapids Challenge

For seasoned rafters only. The Expert Rapids Challenge pushes you through relentless rapids that demand teamwork, strength, and nerve. Our most experienced guides will lead you through some of the most intense whitewater in Virginia. Not for the faint of heart.

  • Difficulty: Expert
  • Duration: 6 hours
  • Min. Age: 16+
  • Group Size: Up to 6
  • Price: $120 per person
