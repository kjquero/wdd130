Columbus, Ohio: A Personal Favorite

Among the cities I have never been to, Columbus, Ohio is one of my favorites. It has the energy of a growing metropolis but also has the warmth and charm of the Midwest. It's urban but doesn't seem to be suffocating. It represents the kind of place I'd love to live in: big enough to have energy and opportunity, but without the overwhelming chaos of a massive metropolis.

From what I've learned, Columbus has a strong sense of community, a growing job market, and a welcoming feel that makes it easy to imagine calling it home. It's the kind of city where you can enjoy a comfortable, balanced life. Not too fast, not too slow.
